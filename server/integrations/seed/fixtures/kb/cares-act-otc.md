# CARES Act section 3702: over-the-counter medicines and menstrual products

Section 3702 of the Coronavirus Aid, Relief, and Economic Security (CARES) Act changed the rules for health savings accounts, health flexible spending arrangements (health FSAs) and health reimbursement arrangements.

For amounts paid after December 31, 2019, over-the-counter medicines and drugs are treated as amounts paid for medical care, and they can be reimbursed from a health FSA without a prescription. Examples include pain relievers such as ibuprofen and acetaminophen, allergy medicine, and cold and flu medicine.

Menstrual care products, such as tampons, pads, liners, cups and sponges, are also treated as amounts paid for medical care.
