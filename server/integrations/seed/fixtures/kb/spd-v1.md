# Health Care FSA Summary Plan Description

Plan: Northwind Analytics Health Care Flexible Spending Account (fictional demo employer). Plan year: January 1, 2026 to December 31, 2026. Version 1, effective January 1, 2026.

## Carryover
Up to $680 of unused health FSA funds remaining at the end of the plan year carry over into the next plan year. Any amount above $680 is forfeited on December 31. The plan has no grace period. Claims for the plan year can be submitted until March 31 of the following year (run-out period).

## Eligible expenses
The plan reimburses medical care expenses as defined in Internal Revenue Code section 213(d) for you, your spouse and your dependents. Over-the-counter medicines and drugs, such as pain relievers, allergy medicine and cold medicine, are eligible without a prescription. Menstrual care products are eligible.

## Ineligible expenses
Vitamins, nutritional supplements and herbal supplements taken for general health are not eligible, unless a medical practitioner recommends them as treatment for a specific diagnosed medical condition and you submit a letter of medical necessity. Cosmetics, toiletries and gym memberships are not eligible.
